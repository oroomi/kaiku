# Try it: /try

Public page with three doors. Door 1 opens /owner. Door 2 takes an email, resets the demo, runs the Christmas Eve chain and sends the real proposal email; the visitor's contact carries channels ["email"], so the ladder logs "blocked: the visitor chose email only" at the WhatsApp and call rungs and stops. Door 3 takes email + phone + "this is my own number" and runs the full ladder to the visitor (WhatsApp only reaches numbers on Meta's allow list, else it is logged as blocked and the call follows).
Guard rails: the visitor's contact always beats the owner's own, and without a phone the email door never falls back to OWNER_WHATSAPP because DC.allows() blocks those rungs. Phone door only 08-22 Helsinki. Caps from data/try_log.jsonl (sha256 + masks only, never raw values): same phone once per Helsinki day, same email 3 per day, 10 phone-door attempts per day. One tester at a time (data/try_lock.json, 150 s); the page counts down and re-enables the button.
Pause switch: KAIKU_TRY_OFF=1 or touch data/try_off gives 503 "paused during the live final" and disables both forms. Status: GET /api/try/status.
Did not work / limits: the 150 s lock is per box, not per visitor, so a reset by another visitor cannot happen mid-test but one slow tester blocks the queue; the daily caps reset at Helsinki midnight and are not a defence against someone typing other people's emails (3 per address per day is the only limit); the live event list under the form is the shared public feed, so it also shows the owner-page tester's events.
