### Approval render: the "rendered 246" hang (7 Nov)
No defect in the composition or assets: all seven clips (A B C D F G P in remotion/public/appr) are valid h264 yuv420p at 30 fps with the planned frame counts, and stills at 240/244/246/248/252 plus a 200-330 range render fine (frame 246 = 8.2 s, 0.6 s into beat A). The stall was environmental: the first render ran under /root/hack/bin/heavy next to other Chromium/Remotion jobs on a 2-core box, was then stopped, and its processes (SIGSTOP'd while the box was shared) kept the progress callback printing the same count every 20 s. Rules for the re-run: browser = chromium_headless_shell-1223/chrome-headless-shell-linux64/chrome-headless-shell (the path chromium-1223/chrome-linux/chrome does not exist), CONC must be <= 2 (renderer refuses 3 on 2 cores), start with `setsid nohup ... &` so a tool-call timeout cannot kill it, never `pkill -f`, and expect to queue behind other heavy jobs (flock).
