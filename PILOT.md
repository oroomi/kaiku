# Pilot plan for Fonecta Connect

## What you get in 8 weeks
| Week | Deliverable |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | Connect Kaiku to a read-only export of exception-hours history and published hours for 50 to 100 consenting customers; agree the scoring method |
| 3-5 | Detect and Suggest running weekly on that data with the confidence gate tuned on your history; WhatsApp message in an approved Finnish template; decide page on your domain; weekly demo |
| 6-8 | Live sends to the pilot customers for real holidays; measured result against today's baseline (how many customers add holiday hours themselves); go or no-go |

## What we need from you
- One owner on your side who spends 1 hour a week.
- Data access: per customer the regular hours, exception hours by date for at least 2 years, category, website, phone number (CSV or API read access).
- A sandbox or staging write path for the Apply step, and an approved WhatsApp sender or template.
- One real decision you will make with it: whether Connect contacts passive customers this way.

## What you get beyond the demo
The same loop for review replies and posts is not built; it would be scoped in week 2.

## Cost
Free pilot for the 8 weeks (our time). From week 9 a price per active customer per month, set after we see how many approve.

## First meeting
30 minutes. Ask: which 50 customers, and can we get their exception-hours history for the last two years?
