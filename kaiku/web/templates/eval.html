HOW WE SCORE OURSELVES

Abstain policy. Below confidence {{ ask_below }} Kaiku asks the owner instead of guessing. A no change answer is a real answer: it counts as a hit when the day really is the regular day. Hit rate counts only rows with an expected value; asks are shown separately.

Three things your scoring script may do differently, and what we would change:

  • It expects hours every time. Set KAIKU_EVAL_ALWAYS_ANSWER=1: the engine then answers below the gate with its best guess (the source's own proposal, else the regular hours), keeps the low confidence number and says so in the reasons.
  • It scores an abstention as a miss. Same flag. Without it the hit rate is shown over all rows and over answered rows, so both readings are visible.
  • It reads one flat hours string. Every row here carries hours_flat (09:00-13:00 or closed, empty for an ask). The Suggestion contract file keeps the structured form; a flat column is one line in the exporter.

Every number on this page is computed by kaiku.suggest.backtest in the request. Method: docs/eval_page.md.
