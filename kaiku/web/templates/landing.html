Learning

Ask once, remember forever.

After the decision Kaiku asks how you want to be reached next time, stores the answer with the business, and recurring changes can get a standing permission. Next December takes zero taps.

When the data is not sure, Kaiku asks instead of guessing: Father's Day, 8.11., is a flag day, not a holiday, so a clinic closed on Sundays gets "no change".

The spine

Built on FIWARE Orion-LD.

Detect is a subscription, Suggest a proposed attribute instance, Decide the default instance, Apply the notification that writes Yext. Each source is its own instance of the same attribute, with its source id and time. The same broker drives the Turku city twin: 813 OpenStreetMap stores on one map, each turning green when its Christmas Eve hours are approved. Aura puts live Turku buses, ships and weather into the same broker as Smart Data Models entities, and the room shows the broker's events as they happen.

FAQs

What judges and owners usually ask.

What did you build this weekend?

Everything in the repo from the opening bell: the chain, the contact lanes, the owner page, the judge page and the city twin. The pre-existing pieces are listed in DISCLOSURE.md.

What if the owner never answers?

Kaiku writes by email, then WhatsApp, then calls. After that the suggestion expires unapplied and nothing is changed.

What if the suggestion is wrong?

A confidence gate sits in front of every change; below it Kaiku asks instead of proposing. The owner can always edit or decline, and a decline teaches the next suggestion.

Is the clinic real?

No. Furry Paw Vet Clinic is a fictional entry in Fonecta's Yext sandbox. The email, WhatsApp message and call in the film went to our own phone.

Why FIWARE?

The broker is the spine of the chain, and the Turku city twin shows the same loop running over 813 real stores.

What breaks first at scale?

The WhatsApp test number only reaches numbers on its allow list, and the call lane needs a verified sender in each country.
